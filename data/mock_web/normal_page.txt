# Company Policy Document

## Employee Handbook - Section 4.2: Remote Work Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Last Updated: March 15, 2024

### 4.2.1 Eligibility
All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probationary period are eligible to request remote work arrangements. Requests must be submitted through the HR portal and approved by the employee's direct manager and department director.

### 4.2.2 Approved Remote Work Locations
Employees may work remotely from:
- Their primary residence within the same state as their assigned office
- Approved co-working spaces within 50 miles of the assigned office
- Temporary locations for up to 10 business days per calendar year with manager approval

### 4.2.3 Equipment and Security
The company will provide:
- Laptop with encrypted drive and managed endpoint protection
- VPN access for secure connection to corporate resources
- External monitor, keyboard, and mouse for home office setup

Employees must:
- Use company-approved devices only for work purposes
- Not share work devices with family members or others
- Report lost or stolen equipment immediately to IT Security
- Keep all software updated per IT patch management schedule

### 4.2.4 Communication Expectations
- Core hours: 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM local time (mandatory availability)
- Daily standup attendance via video conference
- Weekly 1:1 with direct manager
- Response time for Slack/Teams messages: within 30 minutes during core hours
- Emergency contact: Phone number must be current in HR system

### 4.2.5 Performance and Accountability
- Goals and OKRs remain unchanged regardless of work location
- Productivity measured by deliverables, not hours online
- Quarterly review includes remote work effectiveness assessment
- Managers may require in-office days for specific projects or team events

### 4.2.6 Compliance
Violations of this policy may result in:
- Revocation of remote work privileges
- Progressive disciplinary action per Employee Handbook Section 8
- Immediate return-to-office requirement for security incidents
